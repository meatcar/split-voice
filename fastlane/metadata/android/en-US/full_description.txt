Talk through a wireless mic and listen through Bluetooth LE Audio headphones in any voice chat. Android does not let you choose this. Split Voice does it with Shizuku, started with wireless debugging. It does not need root.

Split it from the app or a Quick Settings tile, or let it split automatically whenever a voice chat starts.

How it works: Split Voice asks Android to prefer the mic receiver for voice chat input and the LE Audio headphones for voice chat output. It saves a recovery record first, checks every change, and never overrides another app's audio choices.

- No audio recording and no microphone permission
- No internet permission, analytics or accounts
- Clear setup steps and a restore reminder
- Android 12 or later, a USB mic receiver and LE Audio headphones

Split Voice changes Android's audio routing. Restore before you uninstall it.
